Which fix breaks the most attack paths?

{{ info.brand_name }} joins your network paths, scanner findings and reviewed evidence into routes from an entry point to the systems you must protect. It then picks the fixes that cut the most routes within your effort budget, and shows whether that choice still holds if one piece of evidence turns out to be wrong.

Open Attack Paths Enter your network paths

How a decision is made

  1. 1 Attack Paths Each route from an entry point to a crown jewel, labelled supported, conditional, insufficient evidence or contradicted. Every connection links to the record that backs it.
  2. 2 Remediation Lab Set an effort budget. The lab finds the set of fixes that cuts the most routes and compares it, under the same budget, with fixing by CVSS, EPSS, KEV or asset criticality.
  3. 3 Robustness Withdraw a claim, mark a connection as unknown, or treat a finding as possibly not applicable. You see whether the recommended fix changes, and which piece of evidence caused it.

What it does not do

  • No WAF, IDS or SIEM logs needed. It works from topology, findings and the evidence you record.
  • No scanning or exploits. It never touches your hosts. The only network calls are KEV and EPSS downloads you start.
  • No breach probabilities. A route is a hypothesis to check. CVSS, EPSS and KEV are shown as inputs, not as likelihoods.

Need your own data in it? Load assets.csv and findings.csv on Import, then add connections on Network Paths. The per-CVE exploitation view is under More.
